# Master Services Agreement

Provider and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

## 1. Services and Statements of Work

1.1 **Scope through SOWs.** Provider will perform the services described in one or more statements of work signed or accepted in writing by both Parties (each an "SOW"). Each SOW will describe the services, deliverables, number of issues or requests included, fees, timeline and acceptance criteria. An SOW may be accepted by signature, by email confirmation, or by Client paying the invoice or Payment Link that references it.

1.2 **Order of precedence.** If this Agreement and an SOW conflict, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly states that a specific section of this Agreement is being changed for that SOW only.

1.3 **Change orders.** Work outside an SOW's scope requires a change order. Change orders are billed at $150 per hour or at a fixed price agreed in writing (email is enough). Provider will not start out-of-scope work without Client's written approval.

1.4 **Independent contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Provider decides how, when and where to perform the services, and may use subcontractors bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. Provider remains responsible for its subcontractors' work.

1.5 **Marketplace engagements.** If the engagement was arranged through Upwork, Fiverr or a similar marketplace, payments go through that marketplace and its terms control where they conflict with this Agreement. Neither Party will move payment off the marketplace in violation of its terms.

## 2. Fees and Payment

2.1 **Fees.** Client will pay the fees stated in each SOW. Unless the SOW says otherwise:

| Service type | Payment terms |
|---|---|
| Diagnosis / audit | 100% in advance, before work starts |
| Fix sprint or fixed-price build | 50% deposit before work starts; 50% on acceptance (or deemed acceptance) |
| Retainer | Monthly in advance, on the same day each month |
| Change orders | Invoiced on completion, or as stated in the change order |

2.2 **Invoices and late payment.** Invoices are due within [7] days of issue. Provider may pause work on any SOW while an invoice is more than [7] days overdue, and any delivery dates move by the length of the pause. Overdue amounts accrue interest at the lesser of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate allowed by law.

2.3 **Taxes.** Fees exclude sales, use, VAT and similar taxes, which Client pays (other than taxes on Provider's income).

2.4 **Expenses.** Third-party costs needed for the services (for example paid plans, domains or API usage) are paid by Client directly on Client-owned accounts. Provider will not incur reimbursable expenses without prior written approval.

2.5 **Credits and refunds.** Any credit (for example, a diagnosis fee credited toward a sprint) or refund condition is stated in the applicable SOW. Except as stated in an SOW, fees paid are non-refundable.

## 3. Client Responsibilities

Client will:

1. **Own its accounts.** Keep ownership of all accounts used in the services (for example GitHub, Supabase, Stripe, Vercel, Netlify, domain registrar, email and analytics providers). Provider receives collaborator or member access only, which Client may revoke at any time and which will be removed at handover.
2. **Back up.** Maintain current backups of its code, database and storage before work starts and during the engagement. Provider is not responsible for data loss that a reasonable backup would have prevented.
3. **Provide staging access.** Provide access to a staging or development environment where possible. Where Client asks Provider to work against production, Client accepts the added risk and Provider will make changes there only after Client's written approval of each change.
4. **Provide information and decisions.** Provide timely answers, test accounts, and decisions. Provider's deadlines are extended by any delay in Client's responses.
5. **Have authority.** Confirm that it owns, or is authorized to grant access to and allow testing of, every system, codebase and dataset it shares with Provider.
6. **Not re-prompt over fixes without notice.** Understand that regenerating or re-prompting code with an AI app builder (for example Lovable, Bolt, Base44, Replit, v0 or Cursor) can overwrite delivered fixes. Re-fixing overwritten work is a change order unless covered by a retainer SOW.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including source code, credentials, customer data, business plans and pricing, and the findings in any audit report.

4.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party will use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to perform or receive the services, protect it with at least reasonable care, and disclose it only to people who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as this section.

4.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure, is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information, or is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

4.4 **Required disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice (where legally allowed) and reasonable help in seeking protective treatment.

4.5 **Duration.** These obligations last during the Agreement and for [3] years after it ends, and for trade secrets and credentials, for as long as they remain trade secrets or valid credentials.

4.6 **Security findings.** Provider will not publish or share Client's specific security findings. Provider may publish anonymized, aggregated observations (for example "most audited apps had at least one table readable by anonymous users") that do not identify Client or its app.

Until payment in full, Client has a limited license to use the Deliverables for evaluation.

5.2 **Pre-existing tools.** Provider keeps all rights in its pre-existing and independently developed materials, including its audit scripts, scanners, checklists, templates, prompts, know-how and general-purpose code ("Provider Tools"). To the extent any Provider Tools are incorporated into the Deliverables, Provider grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to use, copy and modify them as part of the Deliverables.

5.3 **Third-party and open-source components.** Deliverables may include open-source or third-party components, which are licensed under their own terms.

5.4 **Client materials.** Client keeps all rights in its code, data, content and trademarks. Client grants Provider a limited license to use them only to perform the services.

5.5 **Portfolio.** Provider may name Client as a client and describe the general nature of the work only with Client's written permission (email is enough). Testimonials are used only as Client approves them.

7.4 **Basis of the bargain.** The Parties agree these limits reflect the allocation of risk and are reflected in the fees.

## 8. Indemnity

8.1 **By Client.** Client will defend and indemnify Provider against third-party claims arising from (a) Client's materials, data or instructions, (b) Client's lack of authority to grant access to or allow testing of any system, or (c) Client's or its users' use of the application, including after handover.

8.2 **By Provider.** Provider will defend and indemnify Client against third-party claims that the Deliverables, as delivered by Provider, infringe a U.S. copyright, except to the extent the claim arises from Client materials, third-party or open-source components, or modifications not made by Provider.

8.3 **Process.** The indemnified Party must give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (no settlement may impose obligations on the indemnified Party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 9. Data Protection

9.1 **Minimum access.** Provider will access Client data, including any personal data of Client's users, only as needed to perform the services. Provider prefers staging data or anonymized data, and read-only access, wherever the work allows.

9.2 **No copies after handover.** Provider will not keep copies of Client's production data. Provider will delete any Client data and database exports it holds within [7] days after handover or termination, except that Provider may keep the Deliverables, the audit report and project correspondence for its records under Section 4.

9.3 **Credentials.** Provider will receive credentials only through a secure sharing method (for example a password-manager share link), never store them in chat, email or source code, and delete them at handover. Client should revoke Provider's access and rotate any shared secrets at handover; Provider will provide a checklist.

9.4 **Secret keys and AI tools.** Provider will not place Client's production secret keys (for example a Supabase service-role key or Stripe live secret key) in the environment of an AI coding agent.

9.5 **Incidents.** If Provider becomes aware of unauthorized access to Client data in Provider's possession, Provider will notify Client without undue delay and in any event within [72] hours, and will cooperate in the response.

9.6 **DPA.** If Client needs a data processing addendum, the Parties will sign Provider's DPA, which then forms part of this Agreement.

## 10. Use of AI Tools

10.1 **Disclosure.** Provider uses AI coding assistants (for example Claude Code by Anthropic and similar tools) to analyze code, propose changes and write tests. Client's source code, configuration and related context may be processed by these tools under the vendors' commercial or business terms.

10.2 **Safeguards.** Provider will (a) use vendor plans or settings under which the vendor does not train its models on Client data, where the vendor offers that option, (b) not submit production personal data or production secret keys to AI tools, and (c) have a human review every AI-generated change before it is proposed to Client.

10.3 **Opt-out.** If Client does not want its code processed by a specific AI tool, Client will say so in writing before the SOW starts. Doing so may change the timeline and fees.

## 11. Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for [12] months after it ends, neither Party will directly solicit for employment or engagement any employee or contractor of the other Party who was involved in the services, without the other Party's written consent. General job postings and hires that result from them are not solicitation. This section does not restrict Provider from serving other clients, including clients in the same industry.

## 12. Term and Termination

12.1 **Term.** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until terminated. Each SOW has its own term.

12.2 **Termination for convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW with [14] days' written notice. A retainer SOW may be cancelled with notice before the next billing date and ends at the end of the paid month.

12.3 **Termination for cause.** Either Party may terminate an SOW immediately on written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within [10] days after notice.

12.4 **Effect of termination.** On termination: (a) Client pays for all work performed and non-cancellable costs incurred through the termination date (for a fixed-price SOW, pro-rated by completed issues or milestones, and the deposit is non-refundable once work has started); (b) Provider delivers all completed and in-progress work that has been paid for; (c) Provider's access is removed and credentials deleted under Section 9; and (d) Sections 2, 4, 5, 6.3, 6.4, 7, 8, 9.2, 11, 12.4 and 13 survive.
