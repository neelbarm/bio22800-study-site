# Statement of Work: White-Label Delivery for Agencies

**SOW number:** [SOW-YYYY-NNN]

**Agency:** [AGENCY LEGAL NAME] ("Agency", the Client under the MSA)
**Provider:** [YOUR LEGAL NAME OR BUSINESS ENTITY], d/b/a ShipReady
**Governing agreement:** Master Services Agreement dated [MSA DATE] between Provider and Agency (the "MSA").

This SOW sets the terms on which Provider delivers ShipReady services that Agency resells to its own clients ("End Clients") under Agency's brand. Each individual job is ordered with a **Job Order** (Section 9), which incorporates this SOW and the matching standard SOW (Diagnosis, Fix & Ship Sprint or Retainer) for scope, issue counts, timelines and acceptance.

## 1. Services and wholesale prices

Agency receives **[15 / 20]% off list** on every job ordered under this SOW.

| Service | List price | Wholesale price ([15]% off) | Wholesale price ([20]% off) | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ship-Ready Diagnosis | $399 | $339 | $319 | 48 hours |
| Fix & Ship 5 (up to 5 issues) | $1,500 | $1,275 | $1,200 | 5-7 business days |
| Fix & Ship 10 (up to 10 issues + production deploy) | $2,500 | $2,125 | $2,000 | 7-10 business days |
| Rebuild-grade (adds payments, auth rebuild or multi-tenant) | $4,000 | $3,400 | $3,200 | 10 business days |
| Wire-It-Up add-on | $1,000-$3,000 | list less [15/20]% | list less [15/20]% | 3-7 days |
| Keep retainer (4 requests) | $500/mo | $425/mo | $400/mo | monthly |
| Grow retainer (10 requests + security pass) | $1,000/mo | $850/mo | $800/mo | monthly |
| Priority retainer (48h turnaround) | $1,500/mo | $1,275/mo | $1,200/mo | monthly |
| Change orders (not discounted) | $150/hr | $150/hr | $150/hr | as agreed |

The 15% tier applies to Agency's first 3 paid jobs. The 20% tier applies from Agency's 4th paid job onward, or earlier by written agreement. Change orders are always billed at $150 per hour or a fixed price agreed in writing, with no partner discount. Diagnosis fees paid by Agency are credited toward a sprint for the same End Client app under the same terms as the standard SOWs.

**Prepaid diagnosis 3-pack (optional):** Agency may prepay **$999 for 3 diagnoses**. Each credit covers one diagnosis of one End Client app and is used by a Job Order. Credits expire [90] days after purchase. Unused credits are refundable within [14] days of purchase if none has been used; once the first credit is used, the pack is non-refundable and unused credits lapse at expiry. Each used credit counts as one paid job for the 20% tier and is worth $333 toward a sprint for the same End Client app.

Agency sets its own resale prices to End Clients. Provider will not undercut Agency's quote to an End Client Agency introduced.

## 2. Payment

Agency pays Provider directly; Agency's payment obligations do not depend on whether the End Client pays Agency.

- Diagnosis: 100% in advance.
- Sprints and add-ons: 50% before start, 50% on acceptance.
- Retainers: monthly in advance.

## 3. Branding and communication

1. Provider will deliver reports, Loom videos and handover documents **under Agency's brand** using Agency's logo and template, or unbranded, as Agency chooses per Job Order.
2. Provider will not mention ShipReady to the End Client or market to the End Client directly.
3. **Communication model** (choose per Job Order): [ ] Agency-only (Provider never talks to the End Client) or [ ] Provider joins calls and channels as a member of Agency's team using [NAME/EMAIL ALIAS].
4. Provider will not describe itself as an employee of Agency, and Agency will not represent Provider's work as guaranteeing security.

## 4. Agency responsibilities

1. Have a written agreement with each End Client that (a) gives Agency the right to subcontract and to grant Provider access, (b) confirms the End Client owns or is authorized to test the app, (c) includes a limitation of liability and no-security-guarantee language at least as protective as the MSA, and (d) discloses the use of AI coding tools as described in the MSA.
2. Arrange collaborator access for Provider on End Client-owned accounts (GitHub, Supabase, hosting), preferably to staging. End Clients keep ownership of all accounts.
3. Pass End Client questions and approvals to Provider within [1] business day.
4. Handle billing, sales and account management with the End Client.

## 5. Non-solicitation of End Clients

During this SOW and for [12] months after the last Job Order ends, Provider will not knowingly solicit any End Client introduced by Agency for services of the kind provided under this SOW, unless the End Client approaches Provider unprompted and Agency consents in writing, or Agency stops serving that End Client. This does not restrict Provider's general marketing.

## 6. Confidentiality and data

End Client information and data are Agency's Confidential Information under the MSA. The MSA's data protection, AI tools and credential handling terms apply to End Client data. If an End Client requires a DPA, Provider will sign Provider's DPA as Agency's subprocessor.

## 7. Intellectual property

On Agency's payment in full for a Job Order, the Deliverables for that job are assigned to Agency (or directly to the End Client if Agency directs). Provider keeps its pre-existing tools under the MSA.

## 8. Re-prompts and warranty

The re-prompt overwrite clause in the standard Retainer SOW applies: re-fixing work overwritten by an AI builder re-prompt is covered only under a retainer; otherwise it is a change order. The 14-day post-acceptance defect window in the standard Sprint SOW applies.
