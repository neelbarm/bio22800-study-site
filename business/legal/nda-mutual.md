# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [DATE] between [YOUR LEGAL NAME OR BUSINESS ENTITY], d/b/a ShipReady, of [PROVIDER ADDRESS], and [OTHER PARTY LEGAL NAME], of [OTHER PARTY ADDRESS] (each a "Party").

## 1. Purpose

The Parties want to share information to evaluate and, if they choose, carry out a software services engagement, including review of the other Party's application, code and infrastructure (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient"), in any form, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential. It includes source code, credentials and API keys, security findings, customer and user data, product plans, pricing and business information.

It does not include information that the Recipient can show (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the Recipient, (b) was known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's information, or (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

## 3. Obligations

The Recipient will:

1. Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose.
2. Protect it with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and no less than reasonable care.
3. Share it only with its employees, contractors and advisors who need it for the Purpose and are bound by confidentiality duties at least as protective as this Agreement. The Recipient is responsible for their compliance.
4. Not reverse engineer, copy or test any system of the Discloser beyond what the Discloser authorizes in writing.
5. Notify the Discloser promptly of any unauthorized use or disclosure it becomes aware of.

**Credentials.** Credentials and keys must be shared only through a secure sharing method (for example a password-manager share link), not in plain email or chat, and deleted when no longer needed for the Purpose.

**AI tools.** The Discloser acknowledges that ShipReady uses AI coding assistants under their commercial terms to analyze code, with vendor model training on the Discloser's data turned off where the vendor offers that option. No production personal data or production secret keys will be submitted to those tools.

## 4. Required disclosure

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information if required by law or court order, after giving the Discloser prompt notice (where legally allowed) and reasonable help in seeking protection.

## 5. Return or destruction

On request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will return or delete the Discloser's Confidential Information and confirm deletion in writing, except copies kept in routine backups or as required by law, which remain subject to this Agreement.

## 6. No other rights

No license or ownership rights are granted except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose. Confidential Information is provided "as is". Neither Party is obligated to enter into any further agreement.

## 7. Term

This Agreement covers disclosures made during the [12] months after the date above. Obligations last for [3] years after each disclosure, and for trade secrets and credentials, for as long as they remain trade secrets or valid credentials.

## 8. Remedies

Unauthorized disclosure may cause irreparable harm, so the Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy.
