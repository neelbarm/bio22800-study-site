# Unit 14 safety audit

Checked 2026-09-19. These primary sources were used to correct the unit's safety language. The summaries below are original notes, not quotations.

- [AIUM Statement on Biological Effects of Ultrasound In Vivo](https://www.aium.org/resources/official-statements/view/statement-on-biological-effects-of-ultrasound-in-vivo): preclinical effects can occur at diagnostic exposure conditions; TI is model-based and can underestimate temperature rise; use TIS through 10 weeks LMP and TIB thereafter in pregnancy.
- [AIUM Prudent Clinical Use and Safety of Diagnostic Ultrasound](https://www.aium.org/resources/official-statements/view/prudent-clinical-use-and-safety-of-diagnostic-ultrasound): in the absence of contrast agents, adverse effects caused by present diagnostic instruments have not been independently confirmed in human patients; use ultrasound prudently and apply ALARA.
- [FDA Information for Manufacturers Seeking Marketing Clearance of Diagnostic Ultrasound Systems and Transducers](https://www.fda.gov/media/71100/download): current clearance guidance references IEC 60601-2-37 for output display and gives application-specific output limits; limits are regulatory ceilings rather than safety targets.
- [AIUM 2019 interpretation of the Output Display Standard](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jum.15159): explains the common capability-above-1 and display-above-0.4 teaching rule and the role of displayed indices in operator decisions.
- [AIUM Safety in Diagnostic Ultrasound Educational Activities](https://www.aium.org/resources/official-statements/view/safety-in-diagnostic-ultrasound-educational-activities-using-nonpregnant-participants): recommended limits differ by tissue and application, reinforcing that 1.9 and 720 mW/cm² are not universal safe targets.
- [AIUM intensity definitions handout](https://aium.s3.amazonaws.com/uls/handouts/17KIS.pdf): supports the spatial and temporal definitions. The guaranteed relationships are SP ≥ SA at a fixed temporal statistic and TP ≥ PA ≥ TA at a fixed spatial statistic; crossed comparisons are not universally rankable.

The legacy 100 mW/cm² unfocused and 1 W/cm² focused figures remain identified only as historical course-recall values. The unit does not present them as current universal safety thresholds.
